Shemiko Privacy Policy

Shemiko Farm Ledger app · Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: Shemiko keeps your farm records on your own phone. There is no account and no Shemiko server. We do not collect, sell or share your information, and the app has no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

Shemiko is made by Elias Zulu in Zambia. If you have a question about this policy or your information, write to eliaszulu74@yahoo.com or call +260 969 928 620.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves what you type or say into it on your phone only, in the app’s own storage. This includes:

  • money you spend and receive, budgets and loans
  • plots and crops, flocks of chickens and ducks, fish ponds and cages, and the daily logs for them
  • jobs, checklists and equipment
  • the names, phone numbers and pay of workers, if you add them
  • your settings, such as language, currency and your own prices

None of this is sent to us. We cannot see it, and we do not keep a copy.

If you add information about workers or other people, please only add what they are happy for you to keep. It stays on your phone like the rest of your records.

Microphone and voice input

The app asks to use the microphone only so you can speak a record instead of typing it, and only listens after you tap the microphone button. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google, on iPhone by Apple). That service may process your voice under its own privacy policy. Shemiko receives only the text, does not record or keep audio, and does not send your voice anywhere itself. You can say no to the microphone and type instead; the rest of the app works the same.

SMS backup

The app can turn your records into text messages that start with “SHM3”, so you can keep a backup. You decide whether to copy or send these messages, and to which number. They travel through your mobile network like any other SMS, which is not encrypted, so only send them to a number you trust.

Internet

The app works without internet. When the phone is online, the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your phone’s internet address, as with any website. The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Children

Shemiko is made for farmers and is not aimed at children under 13.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone’s settings. Because we do not hold a copy, we cannot restore deleted records, so keep an SMS backup if you need one.

Your phone’s screen lock helps protect your records if the phone is lost.

Your rights

You can see, change and delete all your information in the app at any time. Data protection laws, including Zambia’s Data Protection Act of 2021, give you rights over your personal information. Since we do not collect it, there is nothing held by us to request, but you can contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of Shemiko may add features such as an online account, backup to a server, or sharing your record score with a lender or input supplier. Before any such feature sends your information anywhere, we will update this policy, explain what is shared and with whom, and ask for your permission in the app. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
