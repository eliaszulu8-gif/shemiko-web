Delete your Shemiko account

You can ask us to delete your Shemiko account and everything we hold about you at any time. It is free.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to eliaszulu74@yahoo.com from the email address you use to sign in to Shemiko, with the subject “Delete my Shemiko account”.
    If you can’t email, call or WhatsApp +260 969 928 620 and give the email address of your account.
  2. We may reply to check that the request really comes from you.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your account: email address and sign-in details
  • Your profile: name, farm name, place and phone number
  • Your cloud backup: the copy of your farm records (crops, animals, money in and out, jobs and other records)
  • Any licence records linked to your account

What we keep

  • Records of payments for plans, which the law may require us to keep for accounting (up to 7 years). These hold the payment reference, plan and amount, not your farm records.
  • Anonymous daily usage counts. These have no name, email or farm records and cannot be linked to you.

Records on your phone or computer

Farm records saved on your own device are not on our server. To remove them, uninstall the Shemiko app or clear its storage in your phone’s settings (on a computer, clear the website’s data in your browser).

Delete some data but keep your account

You can delete any farm record in the app yourself. To delete only your cloud backup but keep your account, email us with the subject “Delete my Shemiko cloud backup”.
